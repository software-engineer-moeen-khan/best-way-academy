Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about courses, accounts, learning progress, checkout and support.

How do I find a course?

Open the course catalog to search by keyword, browse categories, apply filters and sort available courses. Each course has a dedicated detail page with learning outcomes, curriculum and enrollment options.

Do my cart and wishlist stay after refresh?

Yes. Signed-in learner state is synchronized with the academy platform, while selected interface state may also be cached locally for a faster experience.

How does enrollment work?

Select a course, continue to checkout and complete the enrollment flow. Successful enrollments appear in My Learning and on your learner dashboard.

Does Best Way Academy store my card number?

No. The current checkout payment fields are an interface only and Best Way Academy does not store card numbers. A production payment gateway must handle sensitive payment information securely.

Where is my learning progress saved?

Lesson completion and enrollment progress are connected to your signed-in account so you can continue learning from your dashboard and My Learning area.

When do I receive a certificate?

A course certificate becomes available after the required course lessons are completed. You can open it from the learning experience and print or save it as a PDF.

Can instructors manage courses?

Authorized instructors can use Instructor Studio to manage course information, curriculum, communications, learner activity, promotions and performance tools.

How do I get account or course support?

Use the Contact page for account, enrollment or course questions. Email delivery still needs to be connected to a production mail service before messages can be delivered externally.
