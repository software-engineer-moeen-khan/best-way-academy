Frequently asked questions

How do I enroll in a paid course?

Open a course, continue to checkout, apply a coupon if you have one, pay the shown amount through SadaPay and submit your transaction reference. Access is granted after payment verification.

How does the Azadi Sale work?

The website highlights a Rs. 3,000 Azadi Sale saving. Admin can create or manage the active sale coupon from the Coupons section in the Admin Panel.

Will I get lifetime access?

Once your payment is verified and enrollment is active, your purchased course stays available in your learner account according to the academy's course access policy.

Where can I ask for help?

Use the Contact & Support form below. Support requests are stored in the admin support inbox so the team can track and resolve them.
