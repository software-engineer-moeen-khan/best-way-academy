Privacy notice

This page describes the behavior of the current static frontend demo.

Browser storage

The demo uses localStorage in your browser to remember cart items, wishlist items, demo sign-in state and simulated enrollments. This data stays on your device/browser and is not sent to a Best Way Academy backend because no backend is connected in this version.

Payment details

The checkout interface is a visual demo. Card fields are not submitted to or stored by the website.

Contact forms

The current contact form shows a frontend confirmation only and does not transmit messages to a server.

Future production version

When a backend, analytics, authentication or payment provider is connected, this notice should be replaced with a full production privacy policy describing those services and data practices.
