Privacy notice

An overview of how the current Best Way Academy platform handles learner information.

Important

This is a product-stage privacy notice and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before a commercial launch.

Account information

When you create or update an account, the platform may store information such as your name, email address, role and account-related preferences in its application database.

Learning and commerce data

The platform may store course enrollments, learning progress, orders, wishlist items, cart state, reviews, questions, messages and other learner activity needed to provide the service. Some interface state can also be cached in your browser to improve responsiveness.

Payment details

The current payment form is an interface only and Best Way Academy does not store card numbers. Before accepting real payments, a compliant payment provider should process sensitive payment information directly under its own security and privacy controls.

Support messages

Support forms and messaging features may collect the information you submit. External email delivery is not yet configured, so the Contact page should not be treated as a production email-support channel until a mail provider is connected.

Security and retention

Access to account and administration areas is restricted by application authentication and roles. Production deployment should also include an appropriate retention policy, backups, monitoring and security procedures for personal data.

Third-party services

If analytics, payments, email delivery, video hosting or other third-party services are connected, this notice should be updated to identify those providers and explain how they process information.
